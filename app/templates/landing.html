Priced per account, never per seat

Most tools here charge for every person who touches the account. Add a client, add your accountant, and the bill multiplies - which punishes exactly the people who need this most. Your quota belongs to the account. Share access freely; your price doesn't move.

Blank beats a confident guess

If a field isn't on the document, it comes back empty rather than invented. A missing PO number is an inconvenience; a fabricated one is an error you might not catch. Every document also shows the raw output, so you can check anything on screen.

How it works

  • 1Upload a PDF, PNG, JPG or WEBP. A phone photo of a paper receipt is fine.
  • 2It reads the document and pulls out vendor, invoice number, dates, currency, subtotal, tax, shipping, total, and each line item.
  • 3Review it, then export - one row per document for reconciliation, or one row per line item for expense coding.

Please test with non-sensitive or redacted documents for now. This changes when billing moves to the paid tier, and the Privacy Policy will say so when it does.
