How it works

  • 1Upload a PDF, PNG, JPG or WEBP - a phone photo of a paper receipt is fine.
  • 2It reads the document and pulls out vendor, invoice number, dates, currency, subtotal, tax, shipping, total, and each line item.
  • 3Review it, then export to CSV - either one row per document for reconciliation, or one row per line item for expense coding.

What it will not do

If a field genuinely isn't on the document, it comes back blank rather than guessed. A missing purchase-order number is an inconvenience; an invented one is a bookkeeping error you may not catch. Every document also shows the raw extracted output, so you can check anything on screen against what actually came back.

Extraction is automated and not perfect. Review the results before posting them to your books - it's built to save you the typing, not to remove the checking.
